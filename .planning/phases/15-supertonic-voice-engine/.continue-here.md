<context>
The implementation is deliberately conservative: Supertonic is available only when a complete local model directory is provided, and the CompositeTTSEngine keeps Piper/CLI fallback. This avoids breaking the reader on devices where the model is absent. The next engineering choice is not "whether Supertonic works" anymore; it is how to distribute/validate it on Android without making the APK impractical or violating the phase constraints.
</context>
