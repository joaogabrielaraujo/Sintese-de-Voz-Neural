<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Android document provider -> Flutter | User-selected content URI/bytes and filename are untrusted input. |
| EPUB ZIP bytes -> parser | Archive entries and XML/XHTML content may be malformed or unexpectedly encoded. |
| Imported chapter -> TTS pipeline | Parsed text is consumed by existing normalization and synthesis code. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-13-01 | Tampering | EPUB byte importer | high | mitigate | Validate extension, decode the ZIP boundary, require container structure, normalize paths, and surface domain errors before parsing. |
| T-13-02 | Elevation of privilege | Android picker integration | high | mitigate | Use the native user-selected document flow and add no broad storage permission declarations (D-05). |
| T-13-03 | Denial of service | Large or malformed EPUB | medium | mitigate | Parse asynchronously behind loading state, reject malformed archives, and keep synthesis outside the picker callback. |
| T-13-04 | Information disclosure | Imported document metadata/text | low | accept | Content is processed locally for the selected session; persistence and remote transmission are outside this phase. |
</threat_model>
